The Society

01 / About

Founded on the floor of MCM DAV College for Women, FinNexus exists for one reason: to turn commerce from a subject you study into a discipline you practice. We host the discussions, the case rooms, and the competitions that a textbook can't.

Every member leaves with more than a certificate — a sharper read on markets, a habit of asking "where's the money actually moving," and a network of women who think the same way.
