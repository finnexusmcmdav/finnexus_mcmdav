Who We Are

About Finnexus

Finnexus is MCM DAV College for Women's dedicated commerce club — created for students who want to move beyond the textbook and engage with the real world of finance, marketing, and business strategy.

The club first launched in 2020 with the goal of building a space where commerce students could learn by doing: through competitions, quizzes, case studies, and hands-on projects that connect classroom concepts to practical skills.

After a brief pause, Finnexus is being relaunched this session under a new founding team — carrying that original vision forward while bringing fresh energy, ideas, and events to campus. Our aim is to make commerce more collaborative, practical, and genuinely exciting for every student who's part of it.

Why We Exist

Mission & Vision

Mission

The purpose of the society is to create an environment for students where they can enhance their knowledge and can also showcase their talents in areas like organising events, creative skills, communication and marketing, content writing, social media handling, anchoring, pitching to sponsors and many more corporate and life skills.

Vision

FINNEXUS works with a vision to provide a platform to students including the motto of "Where Theory Meets Practical Implementation". The society strives for the progress and intellectual mastery of every commerce student of the college. It ensures that every student is taught critical lessons before stepping into the corporate world.
